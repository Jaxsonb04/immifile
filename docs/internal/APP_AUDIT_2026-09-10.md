## Production error-message fix

Entering an incorrect password during account deletion preserved the account but displayed “Called by client.” In production, Convex redacts the server error and appends a client-stack marker. The shared error formatter removed the prefix and whitespace, then only recognized that marker when preceded by a newline, allowing it to become the displayed message.

Added a regression reproducing the production envelope (observed failing before the fix), stripped the marker at either the beginning or after a newline, and supplied deletion-specific retry guidance. The full test suite, TypeScript, lint, and release validation passed. This fix is client-side and does not change deletion authorization or require a backend change.

Rebuilt and reinstalled the signed Release simulator app, then repeated the wrong-password request against production. The native alert displayed the new guidance and the synthetic email account remained present. Evidence: `screenshots/pro-fixed-password-error.png` and `fixed-password-regression` Maestro output.

## Remaining product limitations

- Case status updates are manual. Automatic USCIS updates and notifications are absent by design.
- Password recovery and email verification are disabled; the app discloses the access limitation.
- Individual timeline-entry editing, historical-date selection, receipt editing, and denied/withdrawn statuses are not exposed.
- Filing preparation, document upload, and community remain disabled, including guarded deep links.
- Full Apple/Google login and social-only account deletion, physical VoiceOver, real lost-network recovery, and mail-client handoff still require physical-device verification on TestFlight before public release.
- Native logs include generated-framework UIKit notices about a future UIScene requirement and focus caching. These are not observed crashes; they remain upstream compatibility follow-up rather than evidence of a clean future-iOS run.
